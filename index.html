Boil • Simmer • Sauté • Roast • Grill • Poach
🍲

To Boil

/bɔɪl/

Español: Hervir (a borbotones)

Cooking food immersed in water or liquid at 100°C (212°F) with rapid, energetic bubbles.

Kitchen command: "Bring the water to a rolling boil!"

Claudia's dish: Boiling potatoes for a traditional Mallorcan salad or pasta.

🥘

To Simmer

/ˈsɪm.ər/

Español: Cocinar a fuego lento / Chup-chup

Cooking in liquid just below boiling temperature (approx. 85°C – 95°C) with small, lazy bubbles.

Kitchen command: "Lower the heat and let the stock simmer!"

Claudia's dish: Simmering a rich fish fumet or sobrassada stew.

🍳

To Sauté

/soʊˈteɪ/

Español: Saltear (a fuego vivo)

Cooking bite-sized ingredients rapidly in a small amount of hot oil or butter, tossing frequently.

Kitchen command: "Sauté the mushrooms until golden brown!"

Claudia's dish: Salteado de setas (wild mushrooms) or squid rings.

🔥

To Roast

/roʊst/

Español: Asar al horno (carnes, pescados, verduras)

Cooking uncovered in an oven using hot, dry circulating air, creating caramelization and crispy skin.

Kitchen command: "Slide the roast lamb into the main oven!"

Claudia's dish: Mallorcan roasted suckling pig (porcella) or roasted peppers.

🥩

To Grill

/ɡrɪl/

Español: Asar a la parrilla / a la brasa / a la plancha

Cooking food directly over a radiant heat source (open fire, charcoal, gas grill or flattop).

Kitchen command: "Fire two grilled ribeyes for Table 6!"

Claudia's dish: Grilled Sóller prawns with flaky sea salt.

🥚

To Poach

/poʊtʃ/

Español: Escalfar / Pochar en líquido suave

Gently submerging delicate ingredients in liquid kept at 70°C – 82°C without bubbling.

Kitchen command: "Poach two farm eggs for the brunch special!"

Claudia's dish: Poached cod in olive oil (bacalao confitado).

Brigade Safety & Movement

Llamadas de seguridad en cocina

"Behind you!"

¡Atrás! / ¡Paso detrás!

"Hot pan coming through!"

¡Sartén caliente, cuidado!

"Sharp knife! Corner!"

¡Cuchillo afilado! ¡Esquina!

"Heard, Chef!" / "Yes, Chef!"

¡Oído, chef! (Entendido y confirmado)

Pro tip: Saying "Heard" prevents duplicate orders and kitchen accidents.
📋

Pass & Order Calling

Pase, comandas y tiempos

"Order in! Table 4, two covers."

¡Entra comanda! Mesa 4, dos personas.

"How long on the sea bass?"

¿Cuánto le falta a la lubina?

"Two minutes out, Chef!"

¡A dos minutos, Chef! (Listo en 2 min)

"We are 86 on the Sóller red prawns!"

"Eighty-six" = ¡Se nos han agotado! / ¡No queda!

"86" is universal kitchen slang meaning an item is sold out.
🍤

Allergies & Doneness

Alergias y puntos de carne

"Gluten-free / Shellfish allergy"

Sin gluten / Alergia al marisco

"Meat temperatures / Doneness:"

Poco hecho, al punto menos, al punto, muy hecho

"Dressing on the side, please."

El aliño / salsa aparte, por favor.

"Special of the day"

El plato o sugerencia del día.

Crucial: Always double check pans for nut and shellfish cross-contamination!
